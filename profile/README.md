# Your voice agent. Your phone number.

**ParrotVoice connects voice agents to phone calls through an ESP32 device paired with your phone.**

[Visit ParrotVoice](https://parrotvoice.app) · [Get started](https://parrotvoice.app/bridge/) · [Read the docs](https://parrotvoice.app/docs/)

## One device, one project

The **ESP32 firmware** makes your board a Bluetooth hands-free device. **ParrotBridge** installs it and hosts the board's connection in a Chrome tab. Together, they connect your phone to ParrotVoice and your voice agent.

### [Explore ParrotVoice Firmware →](https://github.com/ParrotVoiceApp/firmware)

## A quick introduction

- **Your phone stays at the center.** Calls use its existing number and carrier through the Bluetooth connection.
- **Setup starts in the browser.** Use a classic ESP32 / WROOM-32, a USB data cable, and desktop Chrome.
- **The Bridge tab hosts the line.** Keep it open and the computer awake while using the connection.
- **Your agent handles the conversation.** Configure an assistant in the [Console](https://parrotvoice.app/console/) or follow the [MCP guide](https://parrotvoice.app/docs/mcp-integration/).

Product source code is not currently published. If you would like to inspect, customize, or contribute to the firmware and bridge, **[add a 👍 to the shared open-source vote](https://github.com/ParrotVoiceApp/firmware/issues/2)** and tell us what you would build.
